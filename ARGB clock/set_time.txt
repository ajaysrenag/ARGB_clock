====================================================================
               HOW TO SET AND LOCK THE RTC TIME 
====================================================================

STEP 1: UNCOMMENT AND SET THE CURRENT TIME
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open your project's `src/main.cpp` file in VS Code.
2. Locate line 66 inside the `setup()` function:
   // rtc.adjust(DateTime(2026, 6, 17, 16, 04, 0));

3. Remove the two forward slashes (`//`) at the start of the line to uncomment it.
4. Update the numbers to match your current time in 24-HOUR format:
   rtc.adjust(DateTime(YEAR, MONTH, DAY, HOUR, MINUTE, SECOND));

   * Example (8:30 AM):  rtc.adjust(DateTime(2026, 8, 13, 8, 30, 0));
   * Example (6:45 PM):  rtc.adjust(DateTime(2026, 8, 13, 18, 45, 0));


STEP 2: FIRST UPLOAD (WRITE TIME TO RTC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Connect your microcontroller to your computer via USB.
2. Click the Upload button in PlatformIO.
3. Once finished, your clock will display the time you just configured.


STEP 3: COMMENT OUT AND RE-UPLOAD (LOCK TIME)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Return to line 66 in `src/main.cpp`.
2. Put the two forward slashes (`//`) back in front of the line:
   // rtc.adjust(DateTime(2026, 8, 13, 8, 30, 0));

3. Click the Upload button in PlatformIO one final time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY STEP 3 IS IMPORTANT:
Disabling this line prevents the controller from resetting the clock 
to the hardcoded timestamp every time it powers on or reboots. 
The RTC coin battery will now handle background timekeeping independently.
====================================================================
